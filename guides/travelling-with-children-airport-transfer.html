Airport travel guide

Airport transfers with children

Family airport travel is easier when child-seat needs, pushchairs, luggage and timings are discussed before booking.

State every child’s age

Child-restraint requirements and practical arrangements vary by age and vehicle.

Include pushchairs in the luggage count

Folded pushchairs can take substantial boot space.

Allow extra collection time

Loading children, cases and travel equipment can take longer than an adult-only pickup.

Pack essentials separately

Keep medication, travel documents, snacks and essential child items accessible.

This guide provides general planning information. Exact journey and pickup arrangements are confirmed for each booking.
