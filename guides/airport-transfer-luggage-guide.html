Airport transfer luggage guide

Vehicle selection depends on luggage as much as passenger numbers. Accurate information prevents unsafe loading and last-minute vehicle problems.

Count large cases separately

State the number of full-size cases, cabin bags and loose items.

Mention bulky items

Pushchairs, wheelchairs, golf clubs, surfboards, musical instruments and mobility equipment require advance planning.

Remember return-flight shopping

Passengers sometimes return with more luggage than they left with. Allow for this when planning the return collection.

Send a photo when uncertain

For unusual luggage, a photo can help assess the space required.
