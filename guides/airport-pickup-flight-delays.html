Airport travel guide

Airport pickups and flight delays

Flight information helps plan an airport collection, but passengers should still understand the agreed meeting and waiting arrangements.

Provide the correct flight number

The flight number is more useful than only giving the scheduled landing time.

Confirm the arrival terminal

Large airports can have multiple terminals with different pickup locations.

Keep the passenger phone switched on

The driver may need to contact the passenger after landing or baggage reclaim.

Understand waiting and parking terms

Airport parking and extended waiting can create additional costs. These should be agreed before travel.
