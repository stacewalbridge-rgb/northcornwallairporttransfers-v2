STEP 2 — START THE PROFILE
Go to google.com/business and sign in with the owner’s Google account.
Choose Add your business.

Business name:
North Cornwall Airport Transfers

Use this exact spelling consistently on the website, paperwork, invoices and visible branding. Do not add towns, airports or extra keywords unless they are genuinely part of the real-world business name.

STEP 3 — CHOOSE THE MOST ACCURATE CATEGORY
Start typing categories and choose the closest category Google actually offers.
The most likely primary choice is:
Airport shuttle service

If Google does not offer that exact category in the UK interface, choose the most accurate available transport category, such as:
Taxi service

Only add secondary categories for genuine services actually supplied. Do not add irrelevant categories merely for ranking.

STEP 4 — CHOOSE SERVICE-AREA BUSINESS
When asked whether customers can visit a business location, answer No unless:
- the address is permanently staffed during stated opening hours;
- customers genuinely attend there;
- permanent signage is present;
- the premises comply with Google’s storefront rules.

For a home-based or dispatch-based airport-transfer business, hide the address and operate as a service-area business.

Enter the genuine operational address privately for verification. Never use:
- a taxi rank;
- a virtual office;
- a PO box;
- a mailbox;
- an address the business does not control.

COMMON REASONS FOR SUSPENSION
- Keyword-stuffed business name.
- Using a taxi rank, virtual office or false address.
- Showing a home address where customers are not received.
- Duplicate profiles for each town.
- Phone, website, name or address inconsistencies.
- Categories or services the business does not genuinely offer.
- Fake reviews or fake photographs.
- Creating a separate profile that is not genuinely a separate business.
