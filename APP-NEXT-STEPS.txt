APP OPTIONS

The V6 site is already a Progressive Web App (PWA). Customers can install it from supported Android browsers and it opens without normal browser controls.

Recommended stages:
1. PWA now: installable website, call/WhatsApp and quote form.
2. Booking app: customer accounts, saved journeys, live quotes and confirmations.
3. Driver app: jobs, navigation, status updates and proof of pickup.
4. Admin dashboard: bookings, pricing, drivers, emails and reports.
5. Native Android/iPhone publishing after the booking workflow is proven.

Do not pay Apple/Google developer fees until the live website quote process has been tested and real customer requirements are clear.
